Weather

Warm, then cool

Mid-June in the Hudson Valley averages 78°F by day and drops to the high 50s after sunset. Bring a wrap for the dancing.

Rain plan

If the sky turns, everything moves inside the hay barn. We will text every guest by noon that day.
